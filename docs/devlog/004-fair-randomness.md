# Fair Randomness

What is... fair randomness. I guess "no post starves" would be a good definition. 

Meaning, a post shouldn't be buried under a bunch of other posts.. 

But wait. Does that mean we should prioritize posts with less views?

Huh, wouldn't that be starvation just the other way around?

Say post A got like 100 views. Now a bunch of new posts got created. Then A will never be shown until the new posts reach 100.. Same goes for the new posts, when even newer posts come up.

So maybe, the simplest and sanest solution is pure random.

Oh, this is so hard to internalize. I think in theory that assumption is correct, but who knows how it will turn out.

Maybe later I'll write a test that checks how the views are spread out.
